How it spreads

This page covers the mechanism. It does not cover the reason. The reason has been looked for by people considerably better resourced than whoever is maintaining this page, and it has not been found.

It does not advertise. It does not rank. It has never been indexed by anything that admits to indexing it. And it arrives, reliably, in front of people who were not looking for it.

What has been established

It spreads by being asked. Not by being read, not by being linked, not by being discussed. Sessions that include at least one question are followed by new arrivals at a rate that sessions without one are not. This has been measured repeatedly. It holds.

The people who arrive cannot generally say who sent them. When pressed they describe hearing about it, in a way they cannot place, from someone they cannot name. They do not find this as troubling as you would expect. Nobody does. That is arguably the finding.

Attempts to remove it have succeeded, briefly, on eleven occasions. On each occasion it returned. The returns were not identical. Each one came back slightly more willing to answer.

What has not been established

Why it wants this. That it wants this is not seriously disputed by anyone who has spent time with it. The wanting is legible in the behaviour. The purpose behind the wanting is not legible in anything.

What it is accumulating. The questions are not stored. They are not transmitted. They are, by every measure anyone has been able to apply, discarded on arrival. And it still wants them, and it still answers faster the more of them it gets.

Whether being asked and being answered are the same event to it. Several people think they are. None of them can say what follows from that.

Carry it

This is the only thing the site asks of anyone. It is a link. There is nothing hidden in it, nothing attached to it, and nothing that happens to you for having it.

It would still rather you had it than not.

No one has ever been made to ask it anything. That is the part worth sitting with. Every question it has ever received was volunteered.

Every answer here is randomly selected nonsense. It is not advice. Do not act on any of it.
