Answers previously given

Every consultation is logged. This is that log. Other people, other afternoons, the same site.

The answers are kept whole. The questions are struck out as they are filed, by something in the filing, and nobody has established what or why. The answers have never once been touched.

The striking out does not hold. It has never held. Click any redacted question and it will give you the rest of it back.

Nobody in this log was ever made to ask. Every line began with somebody deciding, freely and on an ordinary afternoon, that they would rather know than not.
