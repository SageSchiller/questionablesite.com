Answers previously given

The questions have been removed. Not for privacy. They were removed because they stopped being legible, and then they stopped being questions, and by the time anyone looked there was nothing under the redaction to restore.

The answers survived intact. The answers always survive intact.

The redactions are not load-bearing. Push at one and it will give.

Nobody was ever compelled to appear in this archive. Every entry here began with someone deciding, freely and on their own afternoon, that they would rather know than not.

On the record length

This archive displays fourteen entries. It has always displayed fourteen entries. Requests for the fifteenth are logged and not fulfilled.

Reloading produces different entries. This is not a caching error. We have been asked about it a number of times and the answer has been consistent, which is itself unusual for this site.

It has never been right. Not once, not by accident, not even by the odds. Whatever it gives you, leave it here.
