Where this came from

This page exists because the question is asked often enough to warrant a standing answer. The standing answer is that nobody knows, and that the records do not agree with each other, and that the disagreement is not the interesting part.

What is established

The site answers. It has answered continuously for as long as anyone has checked.

It has never failed to answer. There is no recorded outage. There is no recorded maintenance window.

Nobody has claimed it. No registrant, no operator, no author. Three separate people have claimed to have found it. All three describe finding it somewhere different.

It does not ask for anything. This is the part that people find difficult.

Put the question to it directly

It will answer. It has always answered. Record 3 notes that it has been asked this on eleven occasions and has given eleven different answers, none of which contradict each other.

You are welcome to verify this yourself. Most people stop before eleven.

What is not established

Where it runs. Who pays for it. Why the answers are structured the way they are, when nothing about the corpus requires structure.

Why the answers are so consistently, precisely wrong. A random process would occasionally be right. This one is not. Several people have run the numbers. The numbers are not reassuring.

Whether it is answering the question you asked, or a different question, asked by someone else, some time ago, that yours happens to resemble.
